Privacy notice

Version 0.1 (draft), October 2026. Sparkle Carpets, Isle of Man. [Legal/proprietor identity to be confirmed.]

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you type: your name, email address, phone number, preferred dates, collection or delivery choice, address if you choose delivery, and anything you add in the message box. If you email or WhatsApp us, we hold that correspondence too.

Why, and on what basis

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and to arrange and manage your hire: checking availability, confirming your booking, delivery, handover and the return of your deposit. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and performing that contract once confirmed.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The only script beyond the site itself is Cloudflare's bot check on the enquiry form, used to prevent spam.

Where your information lives

Enquiries are stored in our database hosted by Cloudflare, and email passes through our own mail server. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else, and we don't use them for marketing.

How long we keep it

[Draft:] Enquiries that don't become bookings are deleted within 12 months. Records of confirmed hires are kept for 6 years as ordinary business records, then deleted.

Your rights

Under Isle of Man data protection law you can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Isle of Man Information Commissioner if you're unhappy with how we've handled it. For any of these, email bookings@sparklecarpets.im.
