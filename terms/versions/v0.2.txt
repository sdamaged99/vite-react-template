Equipment hire terms & conditions

 
Version 0.2 (draft), October 2026. Sparkle Carpets is an independent carpet-cleaning equipment hire service operated by Amanda Lawler in Castletown, Isle of Man.

 

 

 
1. What these terms cover

 
These terms govern the hire of our BISSELL Big Green carpet cleaning machine and any accessories supplied with it. This is equipment hire only: you carry out the cleaning yourself. The equipment remains our property at all times.

 

 

 
2. Booking and payment

 
An enquiry is not a booking. Your hire is confirmed only when we confirm it to you in writing (email or WhatsApp), including the dates, price and any delivery charge. Payment is made at handover by the method we agree with you when confirming, normally cash or bank transfer. Prices are those published on sparklecarpets.im at the time we confirm your booking.

4. Your responsibilities during the hire

 

 
Use the machine only as described in the operating instructions provided, indoors, on domestic carpets and upholstery suitable for hot-water extraction.

 
Use only cleaning detergents suitable for the machine; do not use bleach, solvents or washing-up liquid in it.

 
Keep children and pets away from the machine while it is in use, and do not leave it running unattended.

 
Do not attempt repairs or open the machine; if something seems wrong, stop and contact us.

 
Return it at the agreed time, emptied and rinsed as described in the instructions.

If the machine develops a fault during your hire that is not caused by misuse, stop using it and contact us. We will, at your choice where practical, replace the hire time lost or refund the hire charge for the affected period.
