  1. 1.1. Sparkle Carpets provides carpet-cleaning equipment for customers to use themselves. We do not provide a professional carpet-cleaning service.

  3. 1.3. All hires are subject to equipment availability and confirmation by Sparkle Carpets.
  4. 1.4. Customers must be aged 18 or over to hire the equipment.

  1. 6.1. You agree to use the equipment carefully, responsibly and in accordance with the instructions provided.
  2. 6.2. The equipment must only be used for its intended purpose.
  3. 6.3. You must not:
    • Use the equipment outdoors or expose it to rain.
    • Use it on surfaces or materials unsuitable for extraction cleaning.
    • Use unauthorised cleaning chemicals, solvents, bleach or flammable substances.
    • Operate the equipment with damaged cables, plugs or components.
    • Attempt to dismantle, modify or repair the equipment.

  4. 6.4. You are responsible for checking that the carpets, upholstery or other materials you intend to clean are suitable for the equipment and cleaning products used.

  1. 7.1. Equipment is inspected before each hire and should be supplied clean, complete and in working order.

  3. 7.3. The equipment must be returned by the agreed date and time.
  4. 7.4. Before returning it, please:
    • Empty the clean-water and recovery tanks.
    • Rinse the tanks and remove loose dirt or debris.
    • Remove any remaining cleaning solution.
    • Wipe down the exterior where necessary.
    • Return all supplied accessories.
  5. 7.5. A cleaning charge of up to £10 may apply if the equipment is returned excessively dirty and requires cleaning beyond the normal preparation between hires. Any charge must reflect the actual additional cleaning reasonably required.
