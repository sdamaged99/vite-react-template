Good to know

The questions everyone asks before their first hire.

Do you clean the carpets for me?

No, we hire out the machine and you do the cleaning yourself, which is what keeps it affordable. It's easy, and instructions are included.

I've never used one. Is it hard?

Fill the tank, switch on, slow passes, much like vacuuming. We walk you through it at handover.

How do payment and the deposit work?

You pay Amanda directly, normally at handover, in cash or by bank transfer as agreed. The £75 refundable deposit is paid at the same time and normally refunded within 48 hours of return and inspection, subject to the hire terms.

What's included with the hire?

Every hire includes the machine, its stair and upholstery cleaning attachment and one measured starter portion of detergent, at no additional charge. Extra detergent portions are £5 each; add them when you enquire. Please return all supplied accessories with the machine.

Will it fit in my car?

Please check the machine facts above and contact us if you're unsure; our machine guide covers loading it safely. If you'd rather not move it, choose delivery when you enquire.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Please let us know as soon as possible. Cancelling before your hire starts is normally free, and anything you've already paid for a hire that hasn't started is refunded. Changing dates depends on availability. The details are in section 11 of the hire terms.
