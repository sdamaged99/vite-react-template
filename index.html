Questions people ask

Do you clean the carpets for me?

No, we hire out the machine and you do the cleaning yourself, which is what keeps it affordable. It's easy, and instructions are included.

I've never used one. Is it hard?

Not at all. Fill the tank, switch on, slow passes, much like vacuuming. We'll walk you through it at handover.

Will it suit my carpet?

It suits most synthetic domestic carpets and sturdy wool-mix carpets. Take care with pure wool and delicate carpets, and please don't use it on natural plant-fibre coverings such as jute, sisal, seagrass or coir. Test a hidden area first. Results depend on the carpet and its condition, so we can't guarantee that particular stains or marks will come out.

Will it fit in my car?

Please check the size above and contact us if you're unsure; our machine guide covers loading it safely. If you'd rather not move it, choose delivery when you enquire.

Do I need to be a certain age?

Yes, you need to be 18 or over to hire the machine.

What if I need to cancel or change my dates?

Just let us know as soon as you can. Cancelling before your hire starts is normally free, and anything you've already paid for a hire that hasn't started is refunded. Changing dates depends on availability. The details are in section 11 of the hire terms.
