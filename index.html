FAQs

Good to know before you book

Do you clean the carpets for me? +–

No, Sparkle Carpets is an equipment hire service. You hire the machine and do the cleaning yourself, which is what keeps the cost so low. It's easy, and full instructions are included.

I've never used a carpet cleaner. Is it difficult? +–

Not at all. You fill the tank, switch it on and make slow passes over the carpet, a bit like vacuuming. We include a quick-start guide and run through it with you at handover.

Is cleaning solution included? +–

Carpet cleaning solution is available as an optional add-on when you book, so you have everything you need in one go. Pricing TBC

How do payment and the deposit work? +–

You book your dates online and pay at handover by card or bank transfer. A refundable security deposit is taken at the same time and returned in full once the machine comes back clean and undamaged.

Will it fit in my car? +–

Yes, the machine fits in the boot of most hatchbacks and larger cars. If you'd rather not move it yourself, choose delivery when you book.

Can I cancel or change my booking? +–

Yes, bookings can be cancelled or moved in line with the cancellation policy shown when you book. Placeholder policy
